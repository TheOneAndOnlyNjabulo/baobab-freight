# Driver Hours of Service Policy

This document sets out Baobab Freight & Logistics' driving-hours and rest-break rules, aligned with the National
Road Traffic Regulations governing professional drivers of heavy vehicles.

## 1. Maximum driving time

Drivers may not drive for more than 5 continuous hours without a break, and may not accumulate more than 14 hours
of total driving time within any continuous 24-hour period. These limits are enforced through the vehicle
telematics system, which logs continuous driving time and alerts Baobab's operations centre if a driver approaches
either threshold without a logged break.

## 2. Mandatory rest breaks

A minimum 30-minute break is required after every 5 hours of continuous driving. A minimum rest period of 6
continuous hours is required within any 24-hour period before a driver may resume duty. On multi-day cross-border
trips, drivers take this rest period at a designated Baobab depot, partner truck stop, or secure overnight parking
point along the route — never at an unsecured roadside location, particularly on cross-border corridors.

## 3. Two-driver (team) operations

Long-haul routes exceeding a single driver's practical daily range (notably the Zambia corridor, given its
combined Zimbabwe transit and extended distance) are crewed with two drivers where the booking volume justifies it,
allowing continuous vehicle movement within hours-of-service limits while each driver takes their required rest.

## 4. Fatigue management

Drivers reporting fatigue at any point, regardless of remaining logged hours availability, are required to stop and
rest before continuing — Baobab's fatigue policy takes precedence over schedule pressure, and drivers will not be
penalised for stopping due to genuine fatigue. Dispatch will re-plan delivery timing around a fatigue-related stop
rather than pressure a driver to continue.

## 5. Record keeping

Driving hours, break times, and rest periods are logged automatically via the telematics system and retained for
12 months, consistent with record-keeping requirements for professional driving hours and available for inspection
by the relevant road traffic authority on request.

## 6. Interaction with delivery SLAs

Baobab's published transit times (see the Delivery SLA Policy series) are calculated allowing for full compliance
with this hours-of-service policy — the guarantee windows already account for mandatory driver rest, and hours of
service compliance is never treated as grounds for extending a delivery beyond the guaranteed window.
