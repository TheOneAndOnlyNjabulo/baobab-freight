# Delivery SLA — Botswana Corridor

Guaranteed transit windows for the South Africa ↔ Botswana corridor. Penalty terms are set out in the SLA Policy —
General Terms and Penalties document.

## 1. Border crossings

Botswana corridor traffic crosses primarily at Skilpadshek/Pioneer Gate (for Gaborone-bound traffic) or
Groblersbrug/Martin's Drift (for Francistown and northern Botswana traffic). Baobab selects the crossing based on
final destination to minimise transit time. Standard clearance window at either post is 2–5 hours for a fully
documented consignment.

## 2. Guaranteed transit times

- Johannesburg ↔ Gaborone: 1 business day
- Johannesburg ↔ Francistown: 2 business days
- Johannesburg ↔ Maun: 3 business days
- Pretoria ↔ Gaborone: 1 business day
- Cape Town ↔ Gaborone: 4 business days

## 3. Botswana Unified Revenue Service (BURS) clearance

Import clearance into Botswana is handled by the Botswana Unified Revenue Service. As Botswana and South Africa are
both members of the Southern African Customs Union (SACU), goods of South African origin generally move duty-free
between the two countries, though import VAT and administrative clearance still apply. Baobab's Gaborone agent
handles BURS clearance for account clients.

## 4. Livestock and agricultural goods

Shipments of livestock, meat products, or plant material require veterinary/phytosanitary certification in advance
and are subject to inspection at the border by Botswana's Ministry of Agriculture officials. These shipments are
excluded from the standard transit guarantee due to inspection variability and should be booked with an extended
lead time.

## 5. Route condition notes

The Gaborone corridor is generally the most reliable of Baobab's SADC routes, with consistently low congestion
outside of month-end. The Maun route includes a significant unpaved secondary road segment and is weather-dependent
during the November–March rainy season.
