# Driver Safety and Compliance Policy

This document sets out Baobab Freight & Logistics' core driver safety standards. Driving-hours and rest
requirements, and vehicle inspection procedures, are covered in the separate Driver Hours of Service Policy and
Vehicle Inspection Checklist documents.

## 1. Licensing and permits

All Baobab drivers hold a valid Code 14 driving licence for heavy combination vehicles, a Professional Driving
Permit (PrDP) appropriate to the goods category being carried (including the dangerous goods category where
applicable — see the Dangerous Goods Handling Policy series), and, for cross-border routes, a valid passport and
any country-specific driving documentation required at the relevant border.

## 2. Speed and telematics monitoring

Every Baobab vehicle is fitted with a telematics unit tracking speed, harsh braking, harsh acceleration, and
route adherence in real time. Drivers exceeding posted speed limits or Baobab's internal speed cap (which is set
below the legal limit on several route sections with known accident history) are flagged for a coaching
conversation; repeated violations are escalated per Baobab's progressive disciplinary process.

## 3. Alcohol and substance policy

Baobab operates a zero-tolerance policy for alcohol or drug use before or during driving duty. Random breathalyser
testing is conducted at depot sign-on. A positive test result results in immediate removal from duty for that shift
and referral to Baobab's disciplinary process; a confirmed positive result is grounds for dismissal under Baobab's
code of conduct.

## 4. Incident reporting

Any accident, near-miss, or vehicle damage incident, regardless of severity, must be reported to Baobab's
operations centre immediately and followed up with a written incident report within 24 hours. This applies whether
or not the incident is judged to be the driver's fault — under-reporting of near-misses undermines Baobab's ability
to identify and fix recurring risk patterns on specific routes or vehicles.

## 5. Route risk briefing

Drivers assigned to cross-border corridors receive a route risk briefing covering known high-risk sections (border
congestion points, sections with reported criminal activity, weather-affected sections), updated by Baobab's
operations team based on driver feedback and, where available, industry security bulletins for the relevant
corridor.

## 6. Personal protective equipment

Standard PPE (high-visibility vest, safety boots) is mandatory at all times during loading, unloading, and any
roadside stop. Hazard-class-specific PPE requirements for dangerous goods handling are set out in the Dangerous
Goods Handling Policy series.
