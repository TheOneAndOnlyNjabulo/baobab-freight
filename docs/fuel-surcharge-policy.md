# Fuel Surcharge Policy

Baobab Freight & Logistics applies a fuel surcharge to freight rates to reflect movement in diesel prices, rather
than re-negotiating base rates every time the fuel price changes. This document explains how the surcharge is
calculated and applied.

## 1. Why a surcharge rather than a fuel-inclusive rate

Diesel prices in South Africa are set monthly by the Department of Mineral Resources and Energy and can move
significantly month to month. A separate, transparent surcharge lets Baobab keep base freight rates stable while
still recovering actual fuel cost movement, rather than requiring a full rate renegotiation every time diesel
prices change.

## 2. Base fuel reference price

The surcharge is calculated against a base reference diesel price, reviewed annually. The surcharge percentage
moves up or down from a 0% baseline as the actual monthly diesel price moves away from this base reference, on a
published sliding scale available to account clients.

## 3. Surcharge review cycle

The applicable surcharge percentage is reviewed and, where necessary, adjusted on the 1st of each month based on
the average pump price movement for the preceding month, and communicated to account clients in advance via the
client portal and email. Ad hoc/spot bookings are quoted with the current month's surcharge already included in the
quoted rate.

## 4. Corridor-specific adjustments

Cross-border corridors carry an additional surcharge component reflecting fuel price differentials in the
destination country and, for the Zimbabwe corridor specifically, foreign currency settlement costs (see the SLA
Policy — Zimbabwe Corridor document). This corridor component is reviewed separately from the domestic base
surcharge and may change independently of it.

## 5. How the surcharge appears on invoices

The fuel surcharge is itemised as a separate line on every invoice, calculated as a percentage of the base freight
charge for that shipment (excluding other add-on charges such as insurance premiums or customs disbursements, which
are not subject to the surcharge).
