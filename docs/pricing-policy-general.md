# General Pricing Policy

This document explains how Baobab Freight & Logistics structures freight quotes, invoicing, and payment terms.
Fuel surcharge treatment is covered separately in the Fuel Surcharge Policy document.

## 1. Rate basis

Domestic and cross-border road freight rates are quoted based on the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight
(calculated as length × width × height in centimetres, divided by 6000, giving volumetric weight in kilograms),
consistent with standard road freight industry practice. Full truckload (FTL) bookings are quoted per vehicle
regardless of weight/volume, while less-than-truckload (LTL) and consolidated freight are quoted per the
weight/volume basis above.

## 2. What's included in a standard quote

A standard quote includes collection, road transport to the destination, and standard delivery. It does not
include, unless separately requested: customs clearance and brokerage fees, cross-border permit costs, supplementary
cargo insurance, dangerous goods handling surcharge, or waiting time beyond the standard 30-minute free loading/
unloading allowance at collection and delivery points.

## 3. Account clients vs. ad hoc bookings

Account clients (approved on credit terms) receive monthly consolidated invoicing with 30-day payment terms from
invoice date. Ad hoc clients are quoted and invoiced per shipment, payable before collection unless a deposit
arrangement has been agreed. Account status requires a completed credit application and is subject to Baobab's
standard credit vetting process.

## 4. Minimum charges and small-shipment handling

A minimum charge applies to shipments below a set weight/volume threshold, reflecting the fixed cost of collection
and handling regardless of shipment size. Current minimum charge amounts are published in the client rate card and
reviewed annually alongside the base rate schedule.

## 5. Quote validity

Domestic quotes are valid for 30 days from issue. Cross-border quotes are valid for 14 days from issue, given
greater exposure to fuel and currency movement on SADC corridors. A quote does not constitute a booking confirmation
— bookings are only confirmed once collection is scheduled and accepted by Baobab's operations team.

## 6. Waiting time and additional charges

Waiting time beyond the free allowance at collection or delivery is charged per the rate published in the client
rate card. Failed delivery attempts due to consignee unavailability (see the Delivery SLA Policy — General Terms
document) may incur a re-delivery charge for the second and subsequent attempts.
