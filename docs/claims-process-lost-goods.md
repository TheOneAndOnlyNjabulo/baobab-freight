# Lost Goods Claims Process

This document sets out how clients report and claim for goods lost while in Baobab Freight & Logistics' care,
custody, and control — including non-delivery, misrouting, and theft in transit.

## 1. When a shipment is considered lost

A shipment is treated as lost if it has not been delivered within 10 business days of the expiry of the applicable
guaranteed transit window (see the Delivery SLA Policy series) and cannot be located through Baobab's tracking and
depot-network trace process. Shipments still within active transit or held at a border post for customs reasons are
not "lost" for the purposes of this process, even if delayed.

## 2. Initiating a loss trace

Clients should first log a trace request via the client portal or with their account manager, referencing the
waybill number. Baobab's operations team will trace the consignment through depot scan records, driver logs, and
(for cross-border shipments) the partner agent handling the relevant leg. Most "lost" reports are resolved at the
trace stage as a delay rather than an actual loss.

## 3. Formal loss claim

If the trace process does not locate the shipment within 5 business days, the client may submit a formal loss claim
with: the original waybill, commercial invoice or other proof of value, and confirmation that the goods have not
been separately received. Baobab will issue a written loss confirmation once its internal investigation (including,
where relevant, a review with SAPS or the equivalent authority in the country where the loss occurred) is complete.

## 4. Theft in transit

Suspected theft is treated as a loss claim but triggers an additional internal security review and, for South
African legs, a mandatory SAPS case number, which the client or Baobab will obtain and reference on the claim file.
Cross-border theft is reported to the relevant country's police service by Baobab's local partner agent.

## 5. Liability limits and settlement

Liability limits for lost goods mirror those in the Damaged Goods Claims Process — capped at the lesser of declared
value or the per-kilogram liability cap in Baobab's standard trading terms, unless supplementary cargo insurance was
purchased. Confirmed loss claims are settled within 30 business days of the loss confirmation being issued.

## 6. Partial loss / short-delivery

Where a consignment arrives with fewer units or lower weight than manifested, this is treated as a partial loss.
The consignee should note the shortage on the POD at delivery and report it within the same 48-hour / 5-business-day
window that applies to damage claims, following the same documentation requirements.
