# General FAQ — Tracking and Delivery

## 1. How do I track my shipment?

Account clients can track shipments via the client portal using the waybill number, which shows collection
confirmation, in-transit depot scans, border crossing status (for cross-border shipments), and delivery
confirmation. Ad hoc clients receive tracking updates via the SMS/email notifications configured at booking.

## 2. When does the delivery window start counting?

The guaranteed transit window starts when the driver signs the collection waybill, not when the booking is placed
or paid for. See the Delivery SLA Policy — General Terms document for full detail on how the guarantee works and
what's excluded from it.

## 3. Can I change the delivery address after booking?

Address changes after collection are possible but may affect the guaranteed transit window, particularly on
cross-border shipments where customs documentation is often prepared against the original destination. Contact
your account manager or Baobab's operations centre as early as possible if a delivery address needs to change.

## 4. What happens if no one is available to receive the delivery?

The driver will attempt delivery at the address and contact number provided at booking. If delivery cannot be
completed, this is logged as a failed delivery attempt; a second attempt is scheduled, which may incur a
re-delivery charge (see the General Pricing Policy document) and is excluded from the SLA guarantee under the
"consignee unavailability" exclusion.

## 5. Do you deliver to rural or farm addresses?

Yes — see the Delivery SLA Policy — Domestic Routes document for how rural/outlying delivery affects transit time,
and note that GPS coordinates should be supplied at booking for addresses without a formal street address.

## 6. Can I collect from a Baobab depot instead of having it delivered?

Yes, depot collection is available on request at time of booking at Baobab's Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and
Nelspruit depots, at no additional charge. Cross-border shipments can similarly be collected from Baobab's partner
agent depot in the destination country rather than delivered door-to-door, where this suits the client's
operations.
