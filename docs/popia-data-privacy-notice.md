# POPIA Data Privacy Notice

Baobab Freight & Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a "responsible party" as defined in the Protection of Personal Information
Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This notice explains what personal information Baobab collects, why, and how it is
protected, in respect of clients, consignees, and website/chatbot users.

## 1. What personal information we collect

In the course of providing freight services, Baobab collects: client and consignee contact details (name, phone
number, email, delivery address), driver identification and vehicle details where relevant to a specific
consignment, and, for account clients, billing and company registration information. Where clients use Baobab's
website chat assistant, the question text submitted and the IP address of the request are logged (see section 5).

## 2. Purpose of processing

Personal information is processed strictly for purposes connected to providing freight and logistics services:
arranging collection and delivery, customs clearance where applicable, invoicing, claims handling, and regulatory
compliance (including retention of customs-related documentation as required by SARS). Baobab does not sell client
or consignee personal information to third parties.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

Personal information necessary to complete a shipment is shared with parties directly involved in fulfilling that
shipment: cross-border partner agents, customs authorities in the relevant country, and, where a claim involves an
insurer, the relevant insurance provider. Baobab requires these parties to handle shared information consistent
with POPIA's conditions for lawful processing.

## 4. Data subject rights

Under POPIA, data subjects have the right to request access to personal information Baobab holds about them, to
request correction of inaccurate information, and to object to processing on reasonable grounds, subject to
Baobab's legitimate need to retain certain records for legal and regulatory compliance (such as customs and tax
record-keeping periods). Requests can be directed to Baobab's Information Officer at privacy@baobabfreight.co.za.

## 5. Website chat assistant data handling

The Baobab Freight Assistant chatbot logs each question, the generated answer, and the requesting IP address, for
the purposes of: enforcing a fair-use rate limit per IP address, and improving the assistant's accuracy over time.
This log is retained separately from client/shipment records and is not linked to a specific client account unless
the user identifies themselves within their question. The chatbot does not require login or account creation, and
does not knowingly process special personal information (as defined under POPIA) through normal use.

## 6. Security measures

Baobab applies reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss,
unauthorised access, and disclosure, including access controls on internal systems and encryption of data in
transit for client-facing digital services.

## 7. Retention

Personal information connected to a shipment is retained for the period required by applicable customs, tax, and
commercial record-keeping obligations (generally 5 years from the relevant transaction date), after which it is
securely deleted or anonymised unless a longer retention period is legally required.

## 8. Complaints

Data subjects who believe their personal information has been processed unlawfully may lodge a complaint with
Baobab's Information Officer in the first instance, and thereafter with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
