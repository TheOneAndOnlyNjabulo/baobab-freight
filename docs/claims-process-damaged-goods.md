# Damaged Goods Claims Process

This document sets out how clients report and claim for goods damaged while in Baobab Freight & Logistics' care,
custody, and control.

## 1. Reporting a damage claim

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery for domestic shipments, or 5 business days for cross-border
SADC shipments, by submitting a claim through the client portal or emailing claims@baobabfreight.co.za with the
waybill number, photographs of the damage, and photographs of the packaging as received. Claims reported after
these windows may be declined unless the client can show the damage was latent (not reasonably discoverable at the
time of delivery).

## 2. Noting damage at delivery

Wherever possible, visible damage should be noted on the proof-of-delivery (POD) document at the time of delivery,
with the driver's acknowledgement. A POD signed "unchecked" or "subject to inspection" preserves the client's right
to claim for damage discovered on unpacking, but a POD signed "received in good order" without qualification
significantly weakens a subsequent damage claim.

## 3. Required documentation

A complete damage claim file includes: the signed waybill/POD, commercial invoice showing the value of the goods,
photographs of both the damaged goods and the packaging, and a written description of the damage. For claims
exceeding R50,000 in declared value, Baobab may appoint an independent assessor before settling the claim.

## 4. Liability limits

Unless the client has purchased supplementary cargo insurance (see the General FAQ — Insurance and Claims for how
to arrange this), Baobab's liability for damage is limited under the standard carriage terms to the lesser of the
declared value on the waybill or a per-kilogram liability cap set out in Baobab's standard trading terms and
conditions, consistent with industry-standard road carrier liability limitations. Clients carrying high-value goods
are strongly encouraged to declare full value and purchase supplementary cover.

## 5. Assessment and settlement timeline

Baobab aims to acknowledge a damage claim within 3 business days and to provide a settlement decision within 20
business days of receiving a complete claim file. Incomplete claim files (missing photographs, missing POD) will
pause this timeline until the client supplies the outstanding information.

## 6. Packaging responsibility

Baobab is not liable for damage arising from inadequate packaging supplied by the client or consignor. Where
packaging is visibly inadequate for the nature of the goods at the point of collection, Baobab's driver may note
this on the collection waybill; goods collected with such a note carry a rebuttable presumption that any resulting
damage was caused by the packaging, not by Baobab's handling.
