# General FAQ — Payment Terms

## 1. What payment terms are available?

Account clients approved on credit terms are invoiced monthly with 30-day payment terms from invoice date. Ad hoc
clients pay per shipment, typically before collection, unless a deposit arrangement has been agreed. See the
General Pricing Policy document for full detail on quoting and invoicing.

## 2. How do I apply for account/credit terms?

Submit a credit application through your Baobab account manager, including company registration and trade
reference details. Applications are subject to Baobab's standard credit vetting process; approval and credit limit
are confirmed in writing before account terms take effect.

## 3. What payment methods are accepted?

Electronic funds transfer (EFT) is the standard payment method for both account and ad hoc clients. Card payment is
available for ad hoc bookings through the client portal for shipments below a set value threshold.

## 4. Does the fuel surcharge change my quoted price after booking?

No — the fuel surcharge applicable at the time of booking is locked into the quote. Surcharge changes take effect
on new bookings from the review date forward, not on shipments already booked. See the Fuel Surcharge Policy
document for how and when the surcharge is reviewed.

## 5. Are customs duties and VAT included in my freight quote?

No — standard freight quotes cover collection and transport only. Customs duty, import VAT, and brokerage fees are
separate and are payable by the client (or disbursed by Baobab against a pre-agreed disbursement arrangement for
account clients). See the Customs and VAT Compliance Guide for how these are calculated.

## 6. What happens if an invoice isn't paid on time?

Overdue account balances may result in a hold on new bookings until the account is brought up to date, and interest
may be charged on overdue amounts per Baobab's standard trading terms and conditions. Contact your account manager
promptly if a payment issue arises — Baobab will generally work with clients on a payment plan rather than
suspending service without warning.
