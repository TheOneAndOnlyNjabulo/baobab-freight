# Vehicle Inspection Checklist

This document sets out Baobab Freight & Logistics' pre-trip and periodic vehicle inspection requirements.

## 1. Daily pre-trip inspection

Every driver completes a pre-trip inspection before departure, logged via Baobab's fleet management app, covering:
tyre condition and pressure, brake function (including trailer brake connection on combination vehicles), lights
and indicators, mirrors, load securing equipment (straps, chains, curtain-side condition), and fluid levels (oil,
coolant, brake fluid). A vehicle failing any safety-critical item on this checklist may not depart until the fault
is rectified or the vehicle is swapped.

## 2. Load securing verification

Before departure, the driver verifies that the load is secured per Baobab's load-securing standard (appropriate to
cargo type — palletised general freight, bulk/loose freight, and dangerous goods each have specific securing
requirements, with dangerous goods segregation additionally governed by the Dangerous Goods Handling Policy
series). Load securing is re-checked after the first 50km of a trip and after any stop where the load may have
shifted.

## 3. Periodic roadworthy and service inspection

Every vehicle undergoes a full roadworthy inspection at intervals set by the National Road Traffic Regulations for
its vehicle category, in addition to Baobab's own internal service schedule, which is set more frequently than the
regulatory minimum. Vehicles due for service or roadworthy renewal are flagged in the fleet management system and
are not assigned to new trips once the due date is reached, until the inspection is completed.

## 4. Cross-border vehicle documentation check

Before a cross-border trip, dispatch verifies the vehicle's Temporary Import Permit history, C-BRTA cross-border
permit validity, and cross-border third-party insurance validity, in addition to the standard pre-trip mechanical
inspection. A vehicle with any expired cross-border document is not dispatched on a cross-border route regardless
of mechanical condition.

## 5. Defect reporting

Any defect identified during a pre-trip inspection, en route, or by the driver at any point is logged in the fleet
management app and routed to Baobab's maintenance team. Defects classified as safety-critical (brakes, steering,
tyres, lighting) ground the vehicle immediately; non-critical defects are scheduled for repair at the next planned
maintenance window.
