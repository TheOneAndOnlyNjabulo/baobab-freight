# Dangerous Goods — Class 8: Corrosive Substances

Class 8 covers substances that, through chemical action, cause visible destruction or irreversible alteration of
skin tissue, or that are severely corrosive to metals. Baobab Freight & Logistics regularly moves Class 8
consignments for the industrial chemical, water treatment, mining reagent, and battery/electronics supply sectors.
This document supplements the Dangerous Goods Handling Policy — Overview with Class 8-specific requirements.

## 1. Packing groups

Class 8 packing group is assigned by the severity and speed of tissue destruction observed at defined exposure
times, per SANS 10228:

- Packing Group I: causes full-thickness destruction of skin tissue within 60 minutes of exposure
- Packing Group II: causes full-thickness destruction of skin tissue within 3 hours (but not within 60 minutes)
- Packing Group III: causes full-thickness destruction within 4 hours to 14 days, or is severely corrosive to steel
  or aluminium surfaces without significant tissue effect

## 2. Common products

Baobab's Class 8 volume is dominated by industrial acids (sulphuric, hydrochloric, nitric) and alkalis (sodium
hydroxide solution) moved for mining and water treatment clients, and wet-cell batteries (containing corrosive
electrolyte) moved for the automotive and industrial power sectors.

## 3. Packaging requirements

Class 8 liquids require UN-approved packaging resistant to the specific corrosive product — packaging compatibility
must be confirmed against the SDS, since a container rated for one acid is not automatically suitable for another.
Baobab requires the shipper's packaging compatibility declaration on file for any Class 8 product not already in
Baobab's approved-product register.

## 4. Wet-cell batteries

Wet-cell (flooded lead-acid) batteries are classified as Class 8 due to their electrolyte and must be shipped
upright, terminals protected against short-circuit, and secured against movement. Baobab's standard palletised
battery-handling procedure applies to all new and used battery shipments; used/damaged batteries require additional
leak-containment packaging and are flagged for manifest review before acceptance.

## 5. Personal protective equipment and handling

Baobab's drivers and depot staff handling Class 8 goods use nitrile gloves and eye protection as standard PPE
during loading and unloading checks. Any leaking or damaged Class 8 packaging identified at collection is refused,
not loaded, consistent with the general packaging inspection rule in the Overview document.

## 6. Spill response

Class 8 spill kits include acid-neutralising and alkali-neutralising absorbent compounds (kept separately, as using
the wrong neutraliser can worsen a spill), acid-resistant gloves, and eye-wash provision. Drivers are trained to
identify whether a spilled product is acidic or alkaline from the SDS before applying any neutralising agent.
