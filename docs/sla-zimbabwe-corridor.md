# Delivery SLA — Zimbabwe Corridor

Guaranteed transit windows for the Beitbridge corridor (South Africa ↔ Zimbabwe). Penalty terms are set out in the
SLA Policy — General Terms and Penalties document.

## 1. Border crossing

All Zimbabwe corridor traffic crosses at Beitbridge, South Africa's busiest land border post. Baobab's transit
times below assume a standard clearance window at Beitbridge of 4–8 hours for a fully documented consignment (see
the Cross-Border Documentation Checklist — Zimbabwe for the required paperwork). Beitbridge congestion, particularly
at month-end and around Zimbabwean public holidays, is common and is treated as a routine risk absorbed within the
buffer built into these transit times, not a force majeure exclusion, unless congestion exceeds 48 hours.

## 2. Guaranteed transit times

- Johannesburg ↔ Harare: 3 business days
- Johannesburg ↔ Bulawayo: 2 business days
- Johannesburg ↔ Mutare: 4 business days
- Durban ↔ Harare: 4 business days
- Pretoria ↔ Bulawayo: 2 business days

## 3. Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) clearance

Import clearance into Zimbabwe is handled by the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority. Baobab's cross-border partner agent in
Beitbridge manages ZIMRA clearance on Baobab's behalf for account clients; ad hoc clients must nominate a Zimbabwean
clearing agent at time of booking. Clearance delays caused by missing ZIMRA-required documentation (import permit,
duty pro forma) are excluded from the SLA guarantee.

## 4. Restricted and permit-controlled goods

Second-hand vehicles, agricultural equipment, and certain electronics require an import permit issued in advance by
the relevant Zimbabwean authority. Shipments of these categories without a permit already in hand at collection are
held at Beitbridge and are excluded from the transit guarantee for the duration of the hold.

## 5. Fuel and currency surcharge note

Zimbabwe corridor rates include a corridor-specific surcharge reflecting fuel costs on the Zimbabwean leg and
foreign currency settlement costs; see the Fuel Surcharge and Pricing Policy for the current surcharge percentage
and how it is calculated.
