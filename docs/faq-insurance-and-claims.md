# General FAQ — Insurance and Claims

## 1. Is my shipment automatically insured?

Every shipment is covered by Baobab's standard carrier liability, which is limited — it is not full insurance. For
full-value cover, purchase supplementary cargo insurance at booking. See the Cargo Insurance and Carrier Liability
document for the difference between the two and how each applies to a claim.

## 2. How do I file a claim for damaged goods?

Report within 48 hours of delivery (domestic) or 5 business days (cross-border) via the client portal or
claims@baobabfreight.co.za, with photographs and the waybill number. Full detail is in the Damaged Goods Claims
Process document.

## 3. How do I report a shipment that hasn't arrived?

Log a trace request first via the client portal — most "missing" shipments are located through the trace process
rather than being genuinely lost. If not located, a formal loss claim can be filed. See the Lost Goods Claims
Process document for the full timeline and requirements.

## 4. How long does a claim take to settle?

Damage and loss claims are acknowledged within 3 business days, with a settlement decision typically within 20
business days of a complete claim file being received (30 business days for confirmed loss claims, from the point
the loss is confirmed). Incomplete claim files pause this timeline.

## 5. Am I entitled to a refund if my delivery is late?

Late deliveries not covered by an SLA exclusion (e.g. border delays beyond the standard buffer, force majeure) are
eligible for a rebate on the freight charge, on a sliding scale by how late the delivery was. See the Delivery SLA
Policy — General Terms document for the exact rebate percentages and how to submit a claim.

## 6. What isn't covered by cargo insurance?

Consequential losses (like lost sales or contractual penalties you owe your own customer), inadequately packaged
goods, and certain excluded categories (cash, some high-value electronics without separate declaration, uninsured
dangerous goods without confirmed cover) are not covered. See the Cargo Insurance and Carrier Liability document
for the full exclusions list.
