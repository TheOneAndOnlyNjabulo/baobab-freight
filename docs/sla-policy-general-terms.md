# Delivery SLA Policy — General Terms and Penalties

This policy sets out Baobab Freight & Logistics' guaranteed transit windows and the penalty terms that apply when a
shipment is delivered outside the guaranteed window. Route-specific transit times are published separately — see
the SLA documents for the Domestic, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Zambia, and Mozambique corridors.

## 1. What "guaranteed" means

A guaranteed transit window is the number of business days from confirmed collection to delivery at the consignee's
address, measured door-to-door. The clock starts once Baobab's driver signs the collection waybill, not from the
time a booking is placed. The clock stops on delivery, evidenced by a signed proof-of-delivery (POD) at the
consignee's premises.

## 2. Exclusions from the guarantee

The guarantee does not apply, and no penalty is payable, where the delay is caused by: border post closures or
unscheduled congestion beyond Baobab's control, incomplete or incorrect customs documentation supplied by the
client, force majeure (civil unrest, extreme weather, national strikes), consignee unavailability at the delivery
address, or dangerous goods shipments awaiting regulatory hold. Baobab will notify the client in writing (email or
the client portal) within 24 hours of becoming aware of a qualifying delay.

## 3. Penalty structure

Where a delay is not covered by an exclusion, the client is entitled to a rebate on the freight charge for that
shipment (not the full invoice, where the shipment was part of a consolidated load) as follows:

- 1 business day late: 10% rebate
- 2 business days late: 25% rebate
- 3 or more business days late: 50% rebate
- 5 or more business days late: client may cancel the booking and receive a full refund of freight charges, without
  prejudice to any separate claim for consequential loss

## 4. Claiming a penalty rebate

Penalty rebates are not automatic. The client (or their nominated logistics contact) must submit a claim via the
client portal or to claims@baobabfreight.co.za within 14 calendar days of delivery, referencing the waybill number.
Baobab will assess the claim against the delivery timestamp on the POD and the collection timestamp on the waybill,
and confirm the outcome within 10 business days.

## 5. Force majeure and border delays

SADC border posts (see the Cross-Border Documentation Checklist series) are the most common source of transit
variance on cross-border lanes. Baobab builds a standard buffer into its published transit times to absorb routine
congestion; the guarantee only fails where actual delay exceeds that buffer. Baobab does not guarantee specific
clearance times at any border post, as these are set by the receiving country's revenue authority, not by Baobab.
