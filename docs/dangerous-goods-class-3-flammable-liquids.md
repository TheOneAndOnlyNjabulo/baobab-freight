# Dangerous Goods — Class 3: Flammable Liquids

Class 3 covers liquids, or mixtures of liquids, that give off flammable vapour at or below 60°C (closed-cup flash
point). This is one of the most frequently shipped hazard classes on Baobab Freight & Logistics' network, covering
products such as fuels, paints, thinners, adhesives, and industrial solvents. This document supplements the
Dangerous Goods Handling Policy — Overview with Class 3-specific requirements.

## 1. Packing groups

Class 3 packing group is assigned by flash point and boiling point:

- Packing Group I: flash point below 23°C and boiling point at or below 35°C (high danger — e.g. some solvents)
- Packing Group II: flash point below 23°C and boiling point above 35°C (medium danger — e.g. most paints,
  thinners, petrol-range products)
- Packing Group III: flash point between 23°C and 60°C (low danger — e.g. diesel, many industrial oils)

The packing group must be confirmed on the SDS and stated on the booking; Baobab does not accept verbal
classification for Class 3 goods.

## 2. Packaging and container requirements

Class 3 liquids must be packed in UN-approved, leak-tested containers rated for the assigned packing group.
Metal or approved plastic drums, jerricans, and IBCs (intermediate bulk containers) bearing valid UN packaging
certification marks are accepted; unmarked or improvised containers are refused at collection regardless of the
product inside.

## 3. Loading and segregation

Class 3 loads must be segregated from Class 5 (oxidizers) and from any source of ignition risk on the vehicle.
Electrical equipment on vehicles carrying bulk Class 3 loads above the exempt quantity threshold must be
intrinsically safe in the load area. Smoking, open flame, and non-essential electronic devices are prohibited
within the load area during loading, transit, and unloading.

## 4. Placarding

Vehicles carrying Class 3 goods above the exempt quantity display the Class 3 flammable liquid placard (red
background, flame symbol) on all four sides, along with the relevant UN number panel for the specific product(s)
loaded.

## 5. Common products and typical use cases

Baobab regularly moves Class 3 consignments for the paint and coatings industry, agricultural chemical suppliers
(solvent-based formulations), and fuel resellers moving packaged product (drummed diesel, lubricants) between
depots. Bulk tanker movement of fuel is handled under a separate bulk liquid transport agreement, not the standard
packaged-goods dangerous goods process described here.

## 6. Spill response

Class 3 spill kits carried on Baobab's hazmat-rated vehicles include absorbent material rated for hydrocarbon and
solvent spills, non-sparking tools, and a fire extinguisher rated for Class B (flammable liquid) fires. Drivers are
trained to prioritise ignition source elimination and vapour dispersal over immediate cleanup in a spill incident.
