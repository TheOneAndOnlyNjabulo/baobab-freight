# Cross-Border Documentation Checklist — Mozambique

Requirements specific to the Lebombo/Ressano Garcia and Kosi Bay crossings, supplementing the Cross-Border
Documentation Checklist — Overview.

## 1. Import documentation required by the Autoridade Tributária (AT)

- Declaration lodged through Mozambique's Janela Única Eletrónica (Single Electronic Window) by Baobab's Maputo
  clearing agent
- Commercial invoice and packing list — Baobab requires these in bilingual (English/Portuguese) or Portuguese
  format, as English-only invoices are a frequent source of query at Mozambican clearance
- Certificate of Origin, where a SADC preference is claimed

## 2. Vehicle documentation

- Temporary Import Permit for the vehicle combination
- Third-party insurance valid in Mozambique
- Toll receipts for the N4/Maputo Corridor toll road, where applicable to the route taken

## 3. Permit-controlled categories

Certain agricultural and forestry products, and used vehicles/equipment, require an import permit issued in advance
by the relevant Mozambican authority. As with other corridors, shipments booked without a required permit already
secured are held at the border and are excluded from the SLA transit guarantee for the duration of the hold.

## 4. Choosing the crossing point

Lebombo/Ressano Garcia (the Maputo Corridor) is used for Maputo and southern Mozambique destinations and is
Baobab's highest-volume Mozambique crossing. Kosi Bay is used for coastal KwaZulu-Natal-origin shipments to southern
Mozambique where it offers a shorter routing than transiting via Gauteng and Lebombo.

## 5. Common documentation errors on this corridor

Invoices submitted in English only, without a Portuguese translation or bilingual format, are the most frequent
cause of query at Mozambican clearance on Baobab's network. Clients on regular Mozambique lanes are encouraged to
adopt a standard bilingual invoice template, which Baobab's brokerage team can provide.
