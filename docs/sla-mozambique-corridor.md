# Delivery SLA — Mozambique Corridor

Guaranteed transit windows for the South Africa ↔ Mozambique corridor. Penalty terms are set out in the SLA
Policy — General Terms and Penalties document.

## 1. Border crossings

Mozambique corridor traffic crosses primarily at Lebombo/Ressano Garcia (on the Maputo Corridor, the busiest route)
or Kosi Bay for coastal KwaZulu-Natal traffic to southern Mozambique. Standard clearance window at Lebombo is 3–6
hours for a fully documented consignment, though this post experiences significant queuing during South African and
Mozambican public holiday periods.

## 2. Guaranteed transit times

- Johannesburg ↔ Maputo: 2 business days
- Johannesburg ↔ Beira: 5 business days
- Nelspruit ↔ Maputo: 1 business day
- Durban ↔ Maputo (via Kosi Bay): 2 business days

## 3. Autoridade Tributária de Moçambique (AT) clearance

Import clearance into Mozambique is handled by the Autoridade Tributária de Moçambique (Mozambique Revenue
Authority) through its Single Electronic Window (Janela Única Eletrónica) system. Baobab's Maputo agent manages AT
clearance for account clients. All commercial invoices must be presented with a Portuguese translation or bilingual
format, as Mozambican customs frequently query English-only documentation.

## 4. Beira corridor note

The Beira route is significantly longer than the Maputo corridor and passes through an additional internal
Mozambican checkpoint structure. It is used primarily for shipments destined for central Mozambique and onward
transit to Malawi or eastern Zimbabwe. Road condition on sections of this route is variable, and the published
transit time includes a larger contingency buffer than the Maputo corridor.

## 5. Port-adjacent freight note

Baobab's Maputo corridor is frequently used for freight moving to or from the Port of Maputo as an alternative to
South African ports for clients in Mpumalanga and Gauteng. Port-related congestion at Maputo itself falls outside
Baobab's road-transport SLA once the consignment has been handed over to port operations, and is excluded from the
delivery guarantee at that handover point.
